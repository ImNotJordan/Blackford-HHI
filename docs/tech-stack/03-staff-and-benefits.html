The staff CRM and the benefits portal

Staff and the people they enroll use separate apps built from the same code and reading the same records, so an enrollment finished on a phone appears in the CRM at once.

apps/staff
Modules A, B, C, F, G, H

Staff CRM

Blackford staff · desktop browsers and iPad Safari

Built with

FrameworkNext.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript
InterfaceTailwind CSS and shadcn/ui on Radix; lucide icons
Lists and searchTanStack Table; filters kept in the URL with nuqs; PostgreSQL full-text and fuzzy search
FormsReact Hook Form with Zod validation
ReportsRecharts
Sign-inIdentity Platform: password plus authenticator app

What it handles

  • Client, group and household records with a full timeline
  • Pipelines, tasks, notes, documents and “my work today”
  • Shared text and email inbox, and bulk sends
  • Onboarding checklists; plan, rate, contribution and eligibility setup
  • Life-event approvals and group renewals
  • Carrier file review and approval
  • Reports, audit viewer, user and automation settings

Safeguards

  • Runs on Cloud Run, behind the load balancer and Cloud Armor
  • Multi-factor sign-in for every staff account
  • Signed out after 30 idle minutes (to confirm)
  • Account managers see only their assigned groups
  • Every view, change and export is logged
02
apps/portal
Modules D, E, F

Benefits portal

Employees and individual clients · any phone or computer, opened from a text or email link

Built with

FrameworkNext.js 16, React 19, TypeScript; designed for phones first
Languagesnext-intl, English and Spanish
Sign-inOne-time code to the phone or email on file; no password to remember
Guided flowStep-by-step enrollment that saves at every step
SigningBuilt-in signing with typed or drawn signatures (signature_pad)
DocumentsConfirmation PDFs produced by the automation engine

What it handles

  • Personal details and dependents
  • Plan comparison with per-paycheck and annual cost
  • Elect or waive each benefit, and name beneficiaries
  • Review, sign and receive a confirmation PDF
  • Life events with proof uploads
  • Service requests: ID cards, changes, claim help

Safeguards

  • Links expire, and each sign-in needs a fresh code
  • Texts carry a link, never health details
  • Uploads: phone camera or files, sent straight to Cloud Storage through short-lived links
  • People see only their own and their dependents' records
  • WCAG 2.1 AA on every screen
