Executive summary
The MVP goes live May 7, 2027, about 5½ months after kickoff. Carrier APIs, ACA
reporting, commissions and AI tools follow in two later releases between fall 2027 and
mid-2028.
Staff manage every client and group in one CRM.
Employees are invited, reminded, enrolled and signed
automatically. Changes and renewals run through the
same flow, and completed enrollments go to carriers
as approved files.
The MVP is planned sprint by sprint. The later releases have date ranges
instead, because carriers, vendors and the open enrollment calendar set
their pace.
The beneﬁts portal is built into the platform, so Blackford owns enrollment
and carrier submissions. Ease, or the current process, stays in place until
the MVP is live. Keeping Ease for enrollment instead would shorten the MVP
by about 6 weeks.
