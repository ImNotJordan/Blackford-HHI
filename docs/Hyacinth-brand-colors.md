# Color system

The palette is taken from the Blackford Scope of Work: a warm near-black ground, a ruby red and an antique gold drawn from the logo leaves, and soft ivory and parchment neutrals for paper surfaces.

## Primary colors

Ink carries most surfaces. Ruby and Gold are the two brand accents.

#16130F

Primary**Hyacinth Ink**`RGB 22 19 15`

Cover pages, dark sections, headings and body text on light backgrounds.

#C8191E

Accent**Hyacinth Ruby**`RGB 200 25 30`

Logo leaf, key milestones, track markers, section letters and the left half of the signature bar.

#A8820F

Accent**Hyacinth Gold**`RGB 168 130 15`

Logo leaf, eyebrow labels, rules, highlights and the right half of the signature bar.

## Supporting colors

#E8D9A0

Highlight**Pale Gold**`RGB 232 217 160`

Large numbers and key figures on Ink backgrounds.

#F7EBC5

Tint**Parchment**`RGB 247 235 197`

Fill for automated steps, callouts and highlighted table cells, with a Gold outline.

#F5F3EE

#8C877D

## Usage rules

**Do** use Ruby and Gold together as the signature bar (about one-third Ruby, two-thirds Gold) at the bottom of covers and footers.

**Do** use Gold for small labels and rules, and Pale Gold for large numbers on dark backgrounds.

**Do** keep text colors to Ink or Ivory, whichever contrasts with the surface.

**Don’t** set body text in Ruby or Gold. Ruby on Ink falls below 4.5:1, so use it for large type, icons and shapes.

**Don’t** place Ruby directly beside Gold at equal weight outside the signature bar; let one lead.

**Don’t** add new hues. Use tints of Gold and Gray for extra chart series.
