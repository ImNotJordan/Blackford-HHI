Operating model

Six service areas. One managed operating layer.

  1. 01

    01 Client Experience

    Intake, communications, scheduling, and status updates that keep clients informed.

  2. 02

    02 Legal Operations

    Records coordination, document organization, and deadline tracking under attorney direction.

  3. 03

    03 Finance + Administration

    Billing support, finance workflows, and the back-office processes behind them.

  4. 04

    04 Growth Operations

    CRM administration, outreach, and research that support new business.

  5. 05

    05 Data + Management Visibility

    Data administration and reporting that show leadership what is moving.

  6. 06

    06 Custom Operating Teams

    A dedicated team built around a workflow specific to your firm.
