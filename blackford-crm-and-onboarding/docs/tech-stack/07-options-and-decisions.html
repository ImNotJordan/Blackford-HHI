Options ruled out, and decisions to confirm

Each option was weighed against the BAA, the data model and the Scope of Work's browser-only design. Five choices remain for the December 11, 2026 sign-off.

Considered and not used

Option Why it is not in the stack
Firestore Covered by Google's BAA, but enrollment data is relational and date-based. Carrier change files, ACA months and coverage rules need SQL, so the platform uses PostgreSQL.
Firebase Authentication, Hosting and App Hosting Outside Google's BAA. Identity Platform, the covered upgrade of Firebase Authentication, and Cloud Run take their place.
Supabase Would work with its HIPAA add-on, which requires the Team plan or higher, but adds a second BAA and cost for what Cloud SQL and Identity Platform already provide.
Native iOS and Android apps The Scope of Work keeps the platform in the browser, which covers phones, iPads and desktops without app store releases. Expo stays available for Release 3 options.
Push notifications Not needed, since texts and email reach people without an app. Firebase Cloud Messaging is also outside Google's BAA.

Decision Recommended Alternative
