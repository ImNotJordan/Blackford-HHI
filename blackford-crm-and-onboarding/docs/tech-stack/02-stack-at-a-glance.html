Four rules hold the stack together

1

One door to the data

Only the core layer reads or writes the database, so every permission check runs every time.

2

Every action logged

Each sign-in, view, change, export, message and signature lands in a hash-chained audit log.

3

No health details in transit

Texts and emails carry secure links; logs, URLs and job queues hold record IDs only.

4

Nothing without a BAA

An outside service receives client data only after its agreement is signed.
