Employer portal

Employer HR contacts · desktop or phone

Built with

AppThe benefits portal app, with HR screens and permissions
Sign-inInvite link, then a one-time code at each sign-in; authenticator app optional (to confirm)
Census uploadCSV or Excel, checked field by field by the automation engine
ProgressLive enrollment progress with TanStack Query
TablesTanStack Table with filters

What it handles

  • Company details, contacts and payroll schedule
  • Census upload with an error list to fix and re-upload
  • Approval of the plan and contribution setup
  • Enrollment progress and a weekly digest
  • Payroll deduction updates after approved changes

Safeguards

  • HR sees only their own group
  • Full Social Security numbers are never shown
  • Every census upload and download is logged

Module D

iPad agent mode

Blackford agents at in-person enrollment meetings · iPad Safari

Built with

FlowThe benefits portal's enrollment flow, in agent mode
StartOpened from the staff CRM under the agent's own multi-factor sign-in
TouchLarge tap targets and on-screen signatures
InstallNothing to install; runs in Safari
Later optionOffline iPad (Release 3 option): an Expo app or an installable web app

What it handles

  • In-person enrollment, one employee at a time
  • Signing on the iPad
  • Each enrollment recorded as agent-assisted, with the agent's name

Safeguards

  • No client data is stored on the iPad
  • The session closes when the employee finishes
  • The agent's actions are logged under their own name
